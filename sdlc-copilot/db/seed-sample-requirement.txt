Title: Customer Loyalty Program Portal

We need to build a customer loyalty program portal for retail customers of our chain.
Customers should be able to sign up online, accumulate points from their purchases
(scanned at the POS or imported from e-commerce orders), redeem points for rewards
from a curated catalog, and view their points balance and transaction history.

Tier system: customers should be promoted between Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers
based on points earned in the last 12 months. Gold tier should unlock additional
perks (free shipping, early access to sales).

Admins should be able to:
- Manage the rewards catalog (add, edit, retire rewards, set point cost)
- View customer profiles and adjust points manually for service recovery cases
- Run reports on enrollment, redemptions, and tier distribution

Non-functional:
- Support 100k customers in year one
- Single-sign-on with the existing identity provider
- Mobile-friendly UI
- Data retention follows GDPR — customers can delete their account and have their
  data removed within 30 days
- All admin actions must be audited
